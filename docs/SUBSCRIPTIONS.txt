========================================================================
BENIHANA BOT - HOW SUBSCRIPTIONS WORK
A simple guide for the owner
========================================================================


WHAT IT DOES, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
------------------------------

Right now your bot is private: only the Telegram IDs you listed can use
it. Subscriptions change that rule. Instead of "is this person on my
list?", the bot asks "does this person have days left?". You give
someone 30 days, they use the bot for 30 days, then it stops letting
them in until you give them more. You are the owner, so you never
expire and you are never charged.

The bot handles ACCESS only. It does NOT take money. You collect
payment however you like - bank transfer, QRIS, cash - and then type
one command to switch the person on. That is deliberate: it means you
can change how you get paid without touching the bot.


========================================================================
PART 1 - TURNING IT ON  (do this once)
========================================================================

STEP 1. Find your own Telegram ID.

  Open your bot in Telegram and send:

      /whoami

  It replies with a number, something like 123456789.
  That number is you. Write it down.


STEP 2. Edit your settings file on PythonAnywhere.

  - Go to pythonanywhere.com and log in
  - Click the "Files" tab
  - Open the file called:  pa_config.py
  - Scroll to the bottom and make sure these two lines are there,
    with YOUR number:

      SUBSCRIPTIONS_ENABLED = "1"
      OWNER_IDS = "123456789"

  If those lines are not in the file yet, just type them at the
  bottom. Then click the green "Save" button.

  IMPORTANT: keep the quote marks. "1" not 1.


STEP 3. Restart the bot.

  - Click the "Web" tab
  - Click the big green "Reload" button
  - Wait for it to finish

  Done. Subscriptions are now live.


STEP 4. Check it worked.

  Send this to your bot:

      /subscription

  You should see:  "Owner - unlimited, never expires."

  If you instead see "Subscription required", stop. Your OWNER_IDS
  number is wrong or was not saved. Go back to Step 2.


========================================================================
PART 2 - THE FOUR COMMANDS YOU WILL USE
========================================================================

These three only work for you, the owner. Nobody else can run them.


  /grant <their id> <days>
  ------------------------
  Give someone access.

      /grant 987654321 30

  That gives user 987654321 thirty days. The bot replies with the
  exact date and time their access ends.

  You can add a label if you want to remember what they paid for:

      /grant 987654321 30 monthly
      /grant 987654321 7 trial


  /revoke <their id>
  ------------------
  Cut someone off immediately.

      /revoke 987654321

  They lose access the second you send it. Use this for refunds or
  if someone is sharing their access.


  /subs
  -----
  See everyone.

      /subs

  Shows a list: who is active (green dot), who has lapsed (white
  dot), and how many days each person has left. Sorted so the ones
  expiring soonest are at the top - that is your "who to chase"
  list.


And this one works for EVERYBODY, including people with no access:


  /subscription
  -------------
  Shows the person their own status - active until when, how many
  days left, or "no active subscription" plus their own ID.


========================================================================
PART 3 - WHAT YOUR CUSTOMER EXPERIENCES
========================================================================

1. They find your bot and send /start or any command.

2. The bot refuses them, and shows them their own ID number:

       Subscription required
       This bot is private. Send your ID to the owner
       to get access:  987654321

3. They send you that number (WhatsApp, Instagram, wherever) and
   pay you.

4. You type:  /grant 987654321 30

5. They can now use the bot. Everything works normally for them.

6. When it gets close to running out, /subscription warns them:
   "Your access ends in 3.4 days."

7. When it runs out, they are refused again and see the same
   message with their ID. They pay again, you grant again.


========================================================================
PART 4 - GETTING PAID
========================================================================

The bot does not touch money. Pick whichever suits you:

  MANUAL (recommended to start)
      They transfer to your bank / QRIS / e-wallet, send you a
      screenshot, you run /grant. Zero setup, zero fees beyond
      your bank's, and you can start today.

  TELEGRAM STARS (later, if you get busy)
      Telegram's built-in payment for digital things. It would
      need extra code adding to the bot. Only worth it once
      granting by hand becomes annoying - realistically past
      20 or 30 customers.

Manual is genuinely fine at small scale. Do not build payment
plumbing you do not need yet.


========================================================================
PART 5 - THINGS WORTH KNOWING
========================================================================

RENEWING EARLY DOES NOT LOSE THEM DAYS.
  If someone has 5 days left and pays for another 30, run
  /grant <id> 30 and they end up with 35, not 30. The bot adds
  time, it never overwrites. Nobody is punished for paying early.

YOU CANNOT LOCK YOURSELF OUT.
  Your access comes from the settings file, not from the
  subscriber list. Even if the subscriber file gets deleted or
  corrupted, you still get in and strangers still do not.

EXPIRY IS AUTOMATIC.
  There is no cleanup job to run. A subscription simply stops
  counting the moment its date passes. Nothing can accidentally
  extend someone.

TURNING IT BACK OFF.
  Set SUBSCRIPTIONS_ENABLED = "0" and Reload. The bot goes back to
  the old behaviour - only ALLOWED_USER_IDS gets in. Your
  subscriber list is kept, so switching back on restores everyone.

WHERE THE DATA LIVES.
  A file called subscriptions.json next to the bot on
  PythonAnywhere. It is deliberately NOT on GitHub - your customer
  list is nobody else's business. Back it up occasionally by
  downloading it from the Files tab.


========================================================================
PART 6 - THE REAL LIMIT, AND IT IS NOT THE CODE
========================================================================

Read this before you sell anything.

Your free accounts have hard ceilings that customers share:

  TWELVE DATA (price feed):  800 requests per day, TOTAL, across
      every user combined. One /signal is a few requests. One
      /scan is about 30. Three active people can finish the daily
      quota before lunch.

  PYTHONANYWHERE (hosting):  roughly 100 seconds of CPU per day
      on the free plan. Every signal costs real CPU.

So the code will happily handle 50 subscribers. Your data plan
will not handle 5.

Before you take money from anybody:

  1. Upgrade PythonAnywhere to the paid tier (about $5/month)
  2. Upgrade or replace the Twelve Data plan

Otherwise your paying customers will get "no data" errors and you
will be refunding them. Charge only what covers those costs plus
your margin, and work out your price per user AFTER you know the
data bill.


========================================================================
PART 7 - IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
========================================================================

"That command is for the owner only" when YOU run /grant
      Your OWNER_IDS does not match your real Telegram ID. Send
      /whoami, compare the number, fix pa_config.py, Reload.

Nothing changed after editing pa_config.py
      You did not Reload. Web tab, green Reload button.

Everyone is locked out including you
      Set SUBSCRIPTIONS_ENABLED = "0", Reload, and you are back
      to normal. Then fix OWNER_IDS and try again.

Someone says they paid but cannot get in
      Ask them to send /subscription and read you the exact ID.
      A single wrong digit in your /grant sends the days to a
      user who does not exist.

You granted the wrong person
      /revoke <wrong id>  then  /grant <right id> <days>
